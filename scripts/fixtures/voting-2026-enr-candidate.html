The initial count of ballots will begin after 8 p.m. (Pacific time) on October 24, 2026.
Results reported on election night are preliminary.

Most ballots are counted on election night, including:

• All ballots cast at district electoral offices and advance voting places

• All ballots cast at assigned voting places on Final Voting Day

• In-district ballots cast at special voting opportunities


Ballots not counted on election night include:

• Vote-by-mail ballots

• Assisted telephone voting ballots

• Absentee ballots cast at special voting opportunities

• Absentee ballots cast on Final Voting Day


These ballots are counted about two weeks after election day. Elections BC conducts additional integrity checks on these ballots to ensure that only eligible voters vote, and that they only vote once.

Read more about counting processes.
